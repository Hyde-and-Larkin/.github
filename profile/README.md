# Hyde & Larkin

Product marketing, written down so people and their agents can use it.

Hyde & Larkin is a small, independent go-to-market studio in Atlanta, run by [Wade Burrell](https://wadeburrell.com). We do product marketing consulting, write about where go-to-market is headed, and publish the playbooks we use as open, agent-readable files.

## Open repos

| Repo | What it is |
|---|---|
| [product-marketing-toolkit](https://github.com/Hyde-and-Larkin/product-marketing-toolkit) | Product marketing skills for AI agents: competitive intel, positioning review, claims audit, launch readiness, enablement, messaging, battlecards, discovery. |
| [product-marketing-interview-kit](https://github.com/Hyde-and-Larkin/product-marketing-interview-kit) | Interview prep for product marketing roles: role decoding, company briefs, story bank, round prep, mock interviews, offers. |

Plain Markdown. Works in Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, ChatGPT, or anything that can read a folder.

## Why it lives on GitHub

An AI setup is a pile of small files that depend on each other: positioning feeds messaging, messaging feeds every writing task. Those files need a history, a way back, and a home that isn't tied to one AI vendor. Developers solved that a long time ago, so we're borrowing their tools.
